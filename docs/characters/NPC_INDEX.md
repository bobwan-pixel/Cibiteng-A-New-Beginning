### Character art production rules
- Do not use one face with recolored hair as a substitute for unique NPCs.
- Vary face shape, eye shape, hairstyle silhouette, hair color, body build, posture, clothing, and accessories.
- Create separate deliverables for portrait art and top-down gameplay sprites.
- Gameplay sprites need four directions and idle/walk cycles; combat-capable characters also need attack, hit, guard, and down states as applicable.
- The character showcase image is a concept overview only. Its tiny embedded sprites and labels are not production exports and do not establish new canon names.
- Keep the Pemuda Pancasila-like group fictional in-game: do not copy real organization logos or claim any real person belongs to it.
