# Story Foundation

This document records the currently established story facts. It is not the complete script. Scene dialogue, chapter order, and exact quest conditions should be written in separate documents as they are approved.

## Premise

Bobwan, age 24, arrives in the Cibiteng area hoping to build a quiet life. Everyday relationships and local responsibilities gradually draw him into a serious investigation involving the death of Rangga and the history of Rangga's family.

The setting is grounded and realistic. There are no supernatural explanations.

## Main investigation: Rangga

- Arga is Rangga's older brother and the actual killer of their parents.
- The murders happened four years before the main events.
- Arga resented the attention and family succession shifting toward Rangga after Rangga's birth.
- Arga staged the deaths to look like a traffic accident.
- Rangga knew about the truth.
- Robet is a detective connected to the investigation. Robet and Bobwan do not know each other at the beginning.
- Arga and Damar cooperate.
- Damar later frames Bobwan after a task connected to Pak RT in Desa Cipta Kasih.

## Framing evidence concepts

The current evidence ideas include:
- Bobwan's hair.
- Bobwan's knife.
- Bobwan's jacket.
- A witness who sees Bobwan and Rangga near the jacket or crime-scene area.
- Arga arriving after an argument.

These are design notes, not yet a finalized evidence sequence. The exact placement, discovery order, reliability, and contradictions must be designed before implementation.
